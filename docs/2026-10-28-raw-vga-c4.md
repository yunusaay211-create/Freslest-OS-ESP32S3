# C4 direct RGB332 diagnosis

The user's TV photo after C3 still shows strong red output. C3 removed generated dithering but did not solve the reported physical color failure. A second inspection found no confirmed palette or post-init LCD GPIO ownership error. Do not claim this diagnostic fixes the tint.

C4 adds a diagnostic at the final RGB332 framebuffer boundary. It writes literal bus codes, bypassing RGB565, PLAYPAL, gamma, endian conversion, cursor composition and the saved TV fit. Normal VGA timing, pins and frame ownership are preserved so its result can be compared against the failing app output.

How to open:
- In VGA mode, Settings > Display > FIRE.
- In either OS or Doom, hold RIGHT while resetting, then release RIGHT. The selected output must already be VGA. BACK during reset still forces LCD recovery.
- The pattern lasts ten seconds; BACK exits early. Normal rendering resumes. It changes no saved settings.

Expected image, marked C4 at the upper left:
- Top bars, left to right: white, yellow, cyan, green, magenta, red, blue, black. Literal codes FF FC 1F 1C E3 E0 03 00.
- Four rows beneath: red, green, blue and neutral-gray intensity steps. RGB332 has only four blue levels, so blue steps repeat in pairs.
- Bottom eight blocks: one physical data bit at a time, D0 through D7. Codes 01 02 04 08 10 20 40 80. D0..D7 map to GPIO41,8,13,14,2,42,40,39. Expected groups are two blue, three green, three red blocks, increasing brightness within each group.
- A black border surrounds the pattern.

Interpretation: a wrong direct pattern locates the failure after application color conversion, in the RGB peripheral configuration, GPIO mapping/loading, resistor ladder, cable/converter or display. It does not by itself prove a hardware fault. A correct direct pattern with wrong Doom output calls for capturing and comparing the application framebuffer/palette next. A new photo of this pattern is required before choosing a further color correction.

Validation covers exact raw output bytes, all eight bit blocks, boot shortcut, no NVS changes, BACK exit, normal frame resumption, stalled-DMA rejection, LCD guard, and native/legacy Display-page entry. Physical TV behavior is unverified here.
